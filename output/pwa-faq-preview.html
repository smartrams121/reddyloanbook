📱 Install on Android (Chrome)
  1. Open Chrome browser and go to the Daily Finance website
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top-right corner
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
  4. Tap "Install" in the confirmation popup
  5. The app icon will appear on your home screen — tap it to open
🌐 Using Samsung Internet Browser
  1. Open the Daily Finance website in Samsung Internet
  2. Tap the menu (☰) at the bottom-right
  3. Tap "Add page to" → "Home screen"
  4. Tap "Add" to confirm
💡 The app will open in full screen like a regular app — no browser address bar!
NEW
🍎 Install on iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open Safari browser (this does NOT work in Chrome on iOS)
  2. Go to the Daily Finance website
  3. Tap the Share button (⬆) at the bottom of the screen
  4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  5. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner
  6. The app icon will appear on your home screen — tap it to open
⚠️ Important: You must use Safari. Chrome, Firefox, and other browsers on iOS do not support "Add to Home Screen" as an app.
NEW

Installing Daily Finance as an app gives you:

  1. Quick access — Open directly from your home screen, no need to type the website address
  2. Full screen — The app opens without the browser address bar, giving more screen space
  3. Faster loading — Common pages are cached for faster access on repeat visits
  4. Offline notice — If your internet is down, you'll see a friendly message instead of a browser error
  5. App-like feel — It appears in your recent apps list and can be switched like any other app
📌 No app store download needed. No storage space used. It's a shortcut to the website that looks and feels like a native app.
